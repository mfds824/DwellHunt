Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

DwellHunt is a real estate marketplace for Sri Lanka. This page explains what information we collect when you use this website, why we collect it, who helps us process it, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

When you book a virtual viewing, we collect your name, email address, WhatsApp number, country, time zone, the property, your preferred date and time, any notes you add, and your consent to booking emails and reminders.

When you send an enquiry, we collect your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (if you give one), the property, the type of enquiry, any preferred viewing date, your message and your consent.

When you list your property as an owner, we collect your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, how you prefer to be contacted, the property's full address (including any unit number), its details, price and terms, the photos you upload, and your confirmations and consent. Your phone number, email, street address and unit number are never shown on the website; the public listing shows only the area you chose and the name you chose (or "Private owner").

When we verify an owner listing, we call or message you on WhatsApp and ask to see one proof of ownership, such as a utility bill, rates notice or deed page. We look at it to confirm the listing and do not copy it into our listing records.

If you connect Telegram as an owner or agent, we store your Telegram chat ID, your Telegram first name and the time you connected, so we can send you enquiries, viewing requests and listing updates and receive your replies.

When you message us on WhatsApp, we receive your WhatsApp number, your WhatsApp profile name, your messages and any photos, voice notes or documents you send. We keep a record of the conversation and pass your messages to the DwellHunt team on Telegram so we can reply to you.

Security checks. Our forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a real person is submitting them. Cloudflare processes technical information from your browser for this check.

On your own device. Saved properties, comparison lists and a short-term count of enquiries you have sent are stored in your browser only. We do not receive them.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you the property details you asked for.
  • To arrange your virtual viewing: agent approval, the calendar invitation, the Google Meet link, reminders before the viewing, and a follow-up afterwards.
  • To check and publish owner listings, send owners screened enquiries and viewing requests, and remind owners before a listing ends.
  • To prioritise replies. We rate enquiries as hot, warm or cold from what you tell us (for example whether you asked for a viewing), so that urgent requests are answered first. This rating is never used to refuse you a service.
  • To keep our forms free of spam and abuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Google (Sheets, Calendar, Meet and Gmail) stores booking and enquiry records, creates viewing invitations and video calls, and sends our emails.
  • Cloudflare runs the secure connection between this website and our booking system, and provides the Turnstile human check.
  • Telegram delivers new enquiries and viewing requests to the DwellHunt team, to agents and to property owners who chose to connect Telegram, and passes your WhatsApp messages to the DwellHunt team.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) carries the WhatsApp messages you send to DwellHunt's WhatsApp number and our replies.
  • Cloudinary stores and serves the property photos that owners upload.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Fonts and map content are loaded from their providers when you open the site.

Your details are shared only with the DwellHunt team and the agent responsible for the property you asked about. When you enquire about an owner's listing, DwellHunt passes on your enquiry; we share your contact details with the owner only when you agree, for example to arrange a viewing.

Video viewings

Virtual viewings take place on Google Meet using the link in your confirmation email. We do not record viewings. Anyone who has the meeting link can join it, so please do not forward your link to others.

How long we keep it

  • Viewing bookings: your name, email, WhatsApp number, country, notes and feedback are deleted automatically 90 days after the booking is closed (completed, cancelled, rejected or expired). We keep the booking reference, property, dates and status for our records.
  • Enquiries: kept for as long as we need them to follow up with you. You can ask us to delete them at any time.
  • Owner listings: kept while your listing is live and for up to 12 months after it ends, so we can help if you list again. After that we delete your contact details and the private address. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Owner and agent Telegram connection: kept until you ask us to disconnect you or your listing or agent records are deleted.
  • WhatsApp messages: kept for as long as we need them to answer you and follow up. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.
  • You can cancel a viewing at any time using the private link in your confirmation email.
  • You can ask us to stop contacting you about a property.
  • Owners can close a listing at any time (for example with the "Rented / sold" button in Telegram) and can ask us to disconnect Telegram.

Listings and title

A "verified owner" badge means DwellHunt spoke to the owner by phone and saw one proof of ownership. It is not a legal title check. DwellHunt does not guarantee title, boundaries or approvals. Before paying any money, have a lawyer check the deed and do a title search.

Google account data

DwellHunt does not ask for access to your Google account. Our booking system uses Google services only through DwellHunt's own Google account, to manage our records, calendar invitations, Meet links and emails. DwellHunt's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Security

Our forms are sent over encrypted connections, protected by human checks and rate limits, and our records are kept in access-restricted accounts. No online service can promise perfect security, so please share only the details we ask for.

Children

DwellHunt is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact us

To ask a question or make a request about your information, message us on WhatsApp (+94 77 015 3678) or Telegram, or send an enquiry on any property page.
