Renting an annex in Colombo: 10 things to check

Updated 18 November 2026

An annex is a self-contained unit that shares land with a main house: upstairs, attached at ground level, or a small separate building. It can be a quiet, good-value home, but living next to the owner brings questions an apartment does not. Check these ten things before you pay anything.

1. Your own entrance

Ask whether you have a separate entrance or walk through the main house or garden. A separate entrance gives you privacy and lets you come and go at any hour.

2. The kitchen

Is there a full kitchen, a pantry or kitchenette, or do you share the owner’s kitchen? If cooking matters to you, see it in person and check the gas or cooker point and the sink.

3. Where the annex sits

Upstairs annexes are often brighter and more private but mean stairs and more heat under the roof. Ground-floor and detached annexes can be cooler but closer to the household.

4. Living near the owner

If the owner lives in the main house, ask about house rules early: visitors, overnight guests, gate closing times, noise, pets, vehicles and use of the garden.

5. Electricity and water meters

Separate meters mean you pay exactly what you use. With shared meters, agree in writing how bills are split, for example a fixed monthly amount or a share of each bill.

6. Advance and deposit

Annexes often ask for several months of rent in advance plus a refundable deposit. Agree how the advance is used against rent and when the deposit is returned, and get a receipt for every payment.

7. Water supply

Check water pressure upstairs, whether there is an overhead tank, and what happens during supply cuts.

8. Parking and gate access

Is there space for your vehicle inside the gate, or only on the road? Who opens the gate at night?

9. A written agreement

Put the rent, advance, deposit, notice period, what is included (water, electricity, internet, furniture) and who pays for repairs in a written agreement signed by both of you.

10. Who you are dealing with

Make sure the person renting it out is the owner or authorised by them. On DwellHunt we verify owners and agents before a listing goes live, but you should still see the property in person before paying.

Common questions

Is renting an annex cheaper than an apartment?

Often, because annexes rarely have lifts, pools or management fees. Compare the total monthly cost, including any shared bills, not just the rent.

Do I need a written rental agreement for an annex?

Yes. A short written agreement protects both you and the owner and makes the advance, deposit and notice period clear.

Can I have visitors in an annex?

Usually, but house rules vary when the owner lives on site. Ask before you agree, and add anything important to the agreement.
